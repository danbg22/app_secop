# Supervisión de gestión jurídica: informe del contratista y revisión por etapas

Scope: Operate. Contractor's periodic execution report (guided assistant), the staged review screen for the legal team and supervisors, and the PDFs the app generates from the structured data. Code-led.

Audience and job: service contractors (often non-technical, on phone or office PC) report what they did each period without filling Word or Excel; reviewers in a configurable chain (e.g. revisión jurídica → supervisor) approve or return with a required observation. Constraints: Flutter web, Firestore Spark, per-entity template (header, quincenal on/off, stages, documents with rules: every period / first two / first only, generated by the system or uploaded).

Unresolved: none blocking. Old supervision data (test only) is not migrated.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The report is a conversation, not a form: one question at a time, answers stacked above as the report being written. Refuses the long scroll of identical field cards and the copy-the-PDF layout.

OWN-WORLD: Contrata Legal's established system — cream ground, white cards with soft offset shadow, navy #0E2646 primary, Inter, pill buttons; state colours only for meaning (green done, amber returned, red missing).

STORY: The contractor sees exactly what is asked now, why, and how much is left; finishes, signs once, sends. Reviewers see the formal report on the left and only their own decision on the right.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top band: report number, period, deadline, progress (step x of y). Below: compact stack of answered steps (label, one-line answer, Editar), then the current question card large and focused, with its input and the primary "Siguiente" at its foot. Returned items lead with the observation.
